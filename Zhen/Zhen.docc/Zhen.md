# ``Zhen``

The Zhen app, and the design system it is built from.

## Overview

Zhen is a SwiftUI front end over zhen-core: devices on the local network, what each is known
by, and what has changed. This catalogue documents the design system the screens are built
from, and the rules that keep them consistent: tokens, components, how a screen is put
together, every empty and partial state, the accessibility floor, and every place the app
deliberately differs from the artboards.

Read in order: <doc:Foundations>, then <doc:Components>, then <doc:BuildingAScreen>. The
artboards themselves are the visual reference throughout.

![The Devices room, dark, as the artboards draw it.](01-devices-dark.png)

How the app fits together beyond the design system — isolation, scanning, the helper — is
in `Docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` in the repository.
